# Assembly

Complete bikes arrive about 85 percent assembled. You need to attach the handlebars, front wheel, pedals, and seatpost. Assembly takes about 30 minutes with the tools included in the box.

## Free first tune-up

Every complete bike includes a **free tune-up within 60 days** at any Wobbly Wheel partner shop. Cables stretch during the first few rides, so shifting and braking may need adjustment.

## Professional assembly

If you would rather not assemble the bike yourself, partner shops will assemble it for a flat $60. Bikes assembled improperly at home are still covered by the frame warranty, but damage caused by the improper assembly is not.
