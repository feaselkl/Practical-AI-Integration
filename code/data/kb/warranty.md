# Warranty

## Frames

Wobbly Wheel frames carry a **lifetime warranty** against manufacturing defects for the original owner. Proof of purchase is required.

## Components

Components we sell, including wheels, hubs, drivetrains, and brakes, carry a **2-year warranty** against manufacturing defects.

## E-bike motors and batteries

E-bike motors and batteries are covered for **2 years or 800 charge cycles**, whichever comes first.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Normal wear: chains, cassettes, brake pads, tires, and grips
- Damage from crashes, misuse, or improper assembly
- Bikes used for commercial rental

## Crash replacement

If you crash and damage a Wobbly Wheel frame, our crash replacement program offers a replacement frame at **40% off** list price, within 3 years of purchase.

## Common warranty issues

A ticking or clicking sound from the rear wheel when coasting, which gets worse over time, is usually a dry or worn freehub. On bikes under 2 years old this is covered. We send a freehub service kit, or the customer can take the bike to a partner shop for a free service.
