# E-Bike Battery Care and Safety

## Safety first

**Stop using the battery immediately** and move it outdoors, away from anything flammable, if it:

- Is swollen or bulging
- Smells like burning plastic or chemicals
- Is too hot to touch after charging or riding
- Has been submerged in water

Then contact support right away. Treat these reports as **urgent**. We arrange a free prepaid hazardous-materials pickup and ship a replacement under warranty. Do not ship a damaged battery yourself; carriers will refuse it.

## Everyday care

- Store the battery at 40 to 60 percent charge if you won't ride for more than two weeks
- Do not charge below 32°F (0°C)
- Use only the charger that came with the bike
- Expect about 800 full charge cycles before capacity drops noticeably
