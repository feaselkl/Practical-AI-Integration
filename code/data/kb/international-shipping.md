# International Shipping

We ship to the United States, **Canada**, and the **European Union**. We do not currently ship anywhere else.

## Duties and taxes

International orders ship with duties unpaid. The customer pays any import duties and taxes on delivery. We cannot refund duties on returned items.

## Restricted items

We cannot ship **e-bike batteries** or complete e-bikes outside the United States. Lithium battery shipping regulations make this impractical for us.

## Delivery estimates

International standard shipping takes 7 to 14 business days. Express is not available for international orders.
