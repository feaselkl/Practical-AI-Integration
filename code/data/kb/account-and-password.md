# Account and Password Help

## Resetting your password

Use the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in page. The reset email arrives within a few minutes. Reset links expire after **30 minutes**; request a new one if yours has expired. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.

## Locked accounts

After **5 failed sign-in attempts**, your account is locked for **15 minutes**. Waiting is the fastest fix; support cannot unlock it sooner.

## Two-factor authentication

If you lost access to your two-factor device, support can remove two-factor authentication after verifying your identity. We will ask for the last four digits of the card on a recent order and the shipping ZIP code. We will never ask for your password.

## Changing your email address

You can change the email address on your account from the Profile page. Order confirmations for existing orders still go to the old address.
