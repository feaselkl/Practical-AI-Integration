# Damaged or Incorrect Shipments

## Report it within 7 days

If an item arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, open a ticket within **7 days of delivery**. Include your order number and photos of the damage and of the shipping box.

## What happens next

For damaged or incorrect items, we ship the replacement **right away**, before you return the original. We send a prepaid label for the damaged item, and you have 30 days to send it back.

Do not ride a bike that arrived with a bent wheel, bent fork, or cracked frame.
