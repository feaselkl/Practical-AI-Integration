# Shipping Times and Costs

## Processing

Orders placed before **2 p.m. Central** on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff ship the next business day.

## Delivery estimates

| Method | Time in transit | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 7 business days | Free on orders over $75, otherwise $8 |
| Express | 2 business days | $19 |
| Bike freight | 5 to 10 business days | Free on complete bikes |

Complete bikes always ship by bike freight, separately from the rest of the order, even when you choose express.

## Late orders

If your order is more than **3 business days past the promised delivery date**, open a ticket with your order number. We will open a trace with the carrier, and if the carrier cannot locate the package within 2 business days, we ship a replacement at no charge.
