# Returns Policy

## What you can return

Most items can be returned within **45 days of delivery** for a refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused and in their original packaging, with all tags attached.

Helmets can be returned only if the retention straps have not been adjusted and the helmet has not been worn outdoors. This is a safety rule: we cannot resell a helmet that may have taken an impact.

## E-bikes

E-bikes can be returned within **30 days of delivery**. Returns of e-bikes that are not defective carry a **15% restocking fee**. E-bikes must have fewer than 25 miles on the odometer.

## What you cannot return

- Custom-built wheels and custom-painted frames
- Tubes, tires, and brake pads that have been installed
- Gift cards
- Clearance items marked "final sale"

## How to start a return

Open a ticket with your order number, the item, and the reason. We email a prepaid return label within one business day. Return shipping is free for exchanges and for defective items. For all other refunds, we deduct **$12** from the refund to cover the label.
