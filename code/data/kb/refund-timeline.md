# Refund Timeline

## When we issue the refund

We issue refunds within **3 business days** of the return arriving at our warehouse. You will get an email when the refund is issued.

## When you see the money

- **Credit and debit cards:** 5 to 10 business days after we issue the refund, depending on your bank
- **PayPal:** 1 to 2 business days
- **Store credit:** immediately, and we add a **10% bonus** if you choose store credit instead of a refund

## Duplicate charges

If you see two identical charges for one order, one of them is usually a pending authorization that your bank will drop within 3 business days. If both charges have posted after 3 business days, open a ticket and we will refund the duplicate the same day.
